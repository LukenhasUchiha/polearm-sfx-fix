## 1.2.0

I restored missing BT4 attack vocalizations and expanded the animation coverage:

- Polearm, staff and spear melee attacks, including selected shield and dual-wield attacks.
- Spear throws, with and without a shield.
- Shove and object throws in the game's Tiny and Medium size categories.

## 1.1.0

This is the first public release I have prepared.

- I am providing ten HUM_MS animation overrides that restore missing weapon sound events and correct one sound-object assignment.
- I have included chronological event ordering, three piercing animation markers, a Vortex installer, source files, build instructions and verification tools.
- I tested the final Vortex installation in my game. I have described the limits of my testing in the README.
- I made this project with an AI. I will not maintain it or release updates; I am sharing the source for anyone who wants to continue it.
